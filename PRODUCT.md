# Product

## Platform

web

Responsive web app. Desktop reference 1440×900 with a side nav; mobile reference 390×844 with a bottom tab bar carrying the same entries.

## Users

aLinha serves three roles. Each persona's own screens optimise for that persona; there is no global winner when trade-offs conflict.

- **Responsável designado (Rui Santos, 47).** Administrative and financial director of an SME, not technical and not a lawyer. Holds the legally required cybersecurity-officer role on top of his main job. Needs to understand in plain language what the company must comply with, follow a prioritised action plan, delegate technical work, and show compliance to management, clients and auditors. Sees everything in his own organisation and nothing from others. Reads risks in read-only mode, and only after the consultant validates the roadmap.
- **Analista (Pedro Almeida, 29).** External IT technician who works for the SME on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Sees only the tasks assigned to him (register technical assets, answer delegated technical questions, connect integrations). Never sees risks.
- **Consultor (Marta Ferreira, 34).** aLinha consultant who looks after about 12 SMEs. Works across a client portfolio. Maintains each client's ISO/IEC 27005 risk register, links mitigations to roadmap actions, validates the roadmap, and reviews AI drafts before clients rely on them.

## Product Purpose

aLinha is a CISO-as-a-Service platform that helps Portuguese SMEs comply with cybersecurity and data-protection law: NIS2 as transposed by Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025 (Regime Jurídico da Cibersegurança) and Regulamento n.º 756/2026, plus GDPR (RGPD) and the AI Act. It assesses maturity, turns gaps into a prioritised roadmap in accessible language, and tracks compliance over time as an ongoing process. Success means an SME without an in-house CISO can meet its obligations and prove it.

This repository is the MS3 (UX) very-high-fidelity prototype, an academic project (PEI, Universidade de Aveiro). It centres on the **Riscos e Ativos** plugin and covers the screens around it: Início, Avaliação (questionnaire and roadmap), Tarefas, Equipa, Integrações, client portfolio, history, and the AI assistant. Its current job is to be demoed to supervisors and used in usability tests, so it must look and behave like the real product.

## Positioning

A human consultant and a platform working together. The SME's designated officer holds the legal role, the consultant provides the expertise, and the platform carries the method. AI assists and humans decide. Compliance outcomes are deterministic: the risk level is computed by rule (probability × impact on a configurable matrix), never picked by hand or generated by AI.

## Operating Context

- **Rui's inventory (Scenario 1).** Rui registers critical services and assets with an owner and a criticality, or assigns the registration to Pedro. The inventory feeds risk analysis and the Lista de Ativos the company must send to the CNCS. Rui answers the maturity questionnaire and can delegate technical questions to Pedro.
- **Marta's risk register (Scenario 2).** Marta reviews a client's inventory, records risks against assets (or the whole company), sets treatment (mitigar, aceitar, evitar, partilhar) and residual risk, and links mitigations to roadmap actions so the roadmap is prioritised by real risk. Then she validates the roadmap.
- **After validation (Scenario 3).** Rui sees the risks in read-only plain language ("O que isto significa para a empresa").
- **AI assistant.** Rui asks for policy drafts. Drafts go to the consultant for review before they count. Marta can also hold her own conversations.
- **History.** Every write produces an audit entry (who, when, what, before/after), shown on asset and risk details and in Histórico.
- **External bodies.** CNCS (MyCiber), CNPD, and supplier integrations that suggest assets and collect evidence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The interface is in European Portuguese (pt-PT), with plain language for people who are neither technical nor lawyers.
- Strict RBAC per role on every screen and action (`src/core/rbac.ts`), enforced in the UI and in the data layer.
- Plugins are isolated: `features/assets` and `features/assessment` talk only through `public.ts` contracts.
- There is no backend. Data is simulated in the browser (`src/api/mock`) behind contracts in `src/api/contracts.ts`, ready to swap for the real Kotlin / Spring Modulith API.
- Every surface needs empty, loading and error states. Connection failure can be simulated from the persona menu.
- Personas switch through a discreet demo control (bottom corner, Alt+P, `?as=`), and a demo script jumps to each scenario.
- Out of scope: real backend, Keycloak/MFA, real email, CNCS/MyCiber integration, real supplier connections, real AI, AI incident notifications (US06), and the full assessment questionnaire.
- Open decisions are listed in README "Decisões a confirmar" (D1–D6: asset types, risk scale, risk visibility for clients, location field, chat entities, structured table replies).

## Brand Commitments

- The name is **aLinha**. The wordmark shows the "L" underlining "inha".
- The voice is calm, plain, and reassuring to a non-specialist. It never alarms and never uses legal jargon without an explanation (glossary terms are underlined and explained).
- The UX takes after Vanta: clean, information-dense but calm, with good tables and side panels for detail, and clear states.
- No generic cybersecurity imagery: no shields, padlocks or hackers.
- All AI content is labelled ("✦ Gerado por IA · apenas informativo"). AI never decides anything, and no AI features are invented beyond the requirements.
- Risk levels always pair an icon with text and never rely on colour alone.

## Product Principles

1. **Plain language before precision theatre.** If Rui can't act on it, it isn't finished.
2. **AI assists, the human decides.** AI output is labelled, and drafts go to the consultant for review before anyone relies on them.
3. **Determinism over judgement for compliance outcomes.** Computed values, such as the risk level, are never editable.
4. **Every action is traceable.** Who, when and what is visible where the user needs it.
5. **Each role sees exactly what it should.** Permissions shape the interface, not just the API.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor. axe checks run in Playwright E2E at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Colour-vision modes (protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia) and theme options persist per user. Status and risk always pair colour with an icon and text.
- Users include non-technical people in their late 40s; usability tests are the current phase.
